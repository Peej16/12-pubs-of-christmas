Put everyone's photos in this folder.

1. Copy a photo in here, e.g. dave.jpg
2. In app.js, find the "people" list and put the file name in that person's photo: "dave.jpg"

Tips:
- Square-ish photos look best (they're cropped into a circle).
- Keep them small (under ~300 KB each) so the app loads fast in the pub.
- Use simple names: lowercase, no spaces, e.g. big-mick.jpg
